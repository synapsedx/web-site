Use Cases

Real-World Applications

B2B E-Commerce Customer Inquiries

Handle product availability, details, and characteristic-based searches automatically. Postmaster creates tickets, checks capabilities, queries stock systems, and routes complex requests to humans for validation.

Insurance Medical Evidence Processing

Classify document types, apply appropriate OCR, analyze free text with LLMs, query customer history, assess document consistency and completeness, then trigger insurance workflows.
