The Bridge Between Paper and Digital

Postmaster serves as the crucial link between paper-based and digital processes, analyzing unstructured documents and extracting the information needed for seamless back-office integration.

Whether it's emails, scanned letters, or other documents, Postmaster can scan an email inbox, identify the client and relevant contract, determine the request type, and deduce the required action - all automatically.
