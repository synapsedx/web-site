Our Mission

We recognize that while LLMs excel at language understanding, they lack reasoning skills and function as "black box" systems unsuitable for traceable business operations.

That's why we've developed a hybrid approach that combines the language capabilities of LLMs with the logical reasoning of inference engines, delivering AI solutions that are both powerful and transparent.

Our Story

From Banking to AI Innovation

Our journey began in the IT sector, developing banking back-office software where we witnessed firsthand the challenges of processing unstructured documents.

Exploring LLM applications revealed a critical need: combining language processing with logical reasoning. This insight led to our hybrid solution - an inference engine working alongside LLMs to deliver accurate, traceable, and reliable AI-powered automation.

The name "Synapse" references neural junctions, symbolizing the dynamic exchange of information between human and IT system partners that we enable.
